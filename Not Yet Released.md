## If you want one early

Ask. Finishing everything released to you and wanting more is exactly the
right reason, and saying *which* card and *why* is most of the argument.
Running ahead of the class on your own is not a rule you are breaking —
it is a conversation worth having with your teacher.

What this list is **not**: a syllabus. A card missing from both this list
and your vault has not been written yet — that is a different absence, and
the agent reading this vault knows the difference.
